🌟 PROJECT OVERVIEW 🌟

🧬 WHAT ARE SIGNAL PEPTIDES?! 🧬

Signal peptides are SHORT but MIGHTY amino acid sequences (14-30 residues) at the N-terminus! They're like MOLECULAR ZIP CODES that tell proteins WHERE TO GO! 📬✨ They direct proteins to the endoplasmic reticulum for secretion or membrane insertion, then GET CHOPPED OFF! ✂️💥

🎯 WHY THIS MATTERS - IT'S HUGE! 🎯
